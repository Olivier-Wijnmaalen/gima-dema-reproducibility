# Expert-review interface

The frozen workflow produced provisional assessments for 31 DEMA indicators.
For structured expert review, one indicator was selected from each of the eight
DEMA subdimensions. The subdimensions were deliberately covered in full; the
indicator within each subdimension was randomly selected. The review occurred
after prompt calibration and did not inform the frozen workflow.

One invited DEMA expert completed the review. The reviewer had participated in
the Togo assessment process and could therefore assess the documentary output
against relevant contextual and implementation knowledge. These links identify
the eight indicator-specific dashboard pages presented for review.

## Review instrument

The expert viewed the selected documentary evidence, the provisional phase and
the generated justification, then assessed the following concepts:

| Item | Evaluation concept | Operational focus |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Indicator relevance | Relevance of the selected documentary evidence to the assessed DEMA indicator |
| Q2 | Evidential sufficiency | Adequacy of the available evidence to substantiate the assigned maturity phase |
| Q3 | Evidence specificity | Extent to which the evidence provides concrete, context-specific information |
| Q4 | Interpretive proportionality | Whether conclusions remain within the scope of the evidence |
| Q5 | Criterion linkage | Clarity and correctness of the relationship between evidence and maturity criteria |
| Q6 | Justification specificity | Identification of relevant actors, policies, systems, practices and implementation conditions |
| Q7 | Practical usefulness | Usefulness for subsequent expert review and stakeholder discussion |
| Q8 | Diagnostic usefulness | Identification of ecosystem strengths, limitations and outstanding evidence gaps |
| Q9 | Overall phase defensibility | Whether the assigned phase is justified by the combined evidence and its interpretation |

## Reproducibility qualification

The URLs point to an externally hosted interface and may not remain available
indefinitely. This repository records the routes used in the study but does not
archive the page contents, expert responses or country-assessment evidence.
Public availability of the interface should not be interpreted as permission to
redistribute its underlying materials.
